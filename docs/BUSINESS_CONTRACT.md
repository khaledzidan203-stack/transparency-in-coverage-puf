# Business scope

Analyze reported insurer/plan claims, denials, appeals, resubmissions, enrollment and disclosure availability for PUF 2026 / experience 2024. Support descriptive comparisons with explicit eligible populations and data-quality context.

The project does not assess clinical quality, wrongful denial, causality or fraud. Issuer and plan grains remain separate. Detailed governed definitions are in the [data contract](DATA_CONTRACT.md), [KPI contract](KPI_CONTRACT.md) and [analytical story](ANALYTICAL_STORY.md).
